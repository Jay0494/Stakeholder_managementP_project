# How I Handle Stakeholders

A short portfolio deck on stakeholder management in a BI and reporting role: how I clarify a decision, map who needs what, tailor each message, check the numbers before they are shown, label what is not yet known, and close with specific asks.

The deck uses a delivery performance review for an operations manager ahead of a decision meeting as its worked example.

## Contents

- `docs/How_I_Handle_Stakeholders.pdf`: the deck, 8 slides, 16:9.
- `docs/index.html`: the same deck as HTML, for viewing in a browser or publishing with GitHub Pages.

## Method in brief

1. Clarify the decision: what will be decided, by whom, and by when.
2. Map the stakeholders: who needs what, and what goes wrong if their need is missed.
3. Tailor each message to its audience.
4. Challenge the numbers: definitions, totals and claims, before anyone sees them.
5. Say what is not yet known, rather than filling gaps with estimates.
6. Close with specific decisions and specific data requests.

## Notes

The worked example is illustrative of the approach. Figures and wording describe the analysis process, not a published company report.
